TOXIC BARRIER / VISUAL PROCESS

01Silhouette first

I sketched a tower that grows with each upgrade,
alongside the four resources used to improve it.

Small on screen. Recognisable at a glance.

Resource shapes stay distinct against the busy ground.
The wood asset remains part of the original set.

02Tower progression

Increasing height and mass make progression visible. Shared stone colours keep the stages related.

03A shared visual language

Approved backgrounds guide new artwork.
I compare palette, lighting and pixel detail
before preparing sprites for Unity.

I use sketches, prompts and style references to generate
artwork with AI, then select and integrate the results.
