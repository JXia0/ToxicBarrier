# Cover generation

Method: built-in image generation tool, with the project's gameplay screenshot, tower sprite, goblin sprite sheet and first-region map as references.

Prompt:

Create a completely new landscape cover illustration for the indie game TOXIC BARRIER. Asset type: pixel-art game portfolio cover background, 2:1 wide composition, target 3000x1500. Use the attached existing game assets as strict design/style reference: image1 actual gameplay for camera, black hooded player and visual proportions; image2 the exact allied upgraded stone tower design; image3 the actual green goblin sprite sheet; image4 actual warm gold ruined courtyard ground. Do not turn this into polished painted fantasy art. Render genuinely crisp, deliberately clustered pixel art with visible square pixels and hard stepped edges, approximately a 750x375 logical pixel canvas enlarged 4x. Match the in-game 2D sprites, limited shading, dark outlines, chipped ochre stone, burgundy vegetation, ivory candles and teal toxic mist. No smooth gradients, no blur, no glossy plastic, no cinematic realism, no smooth vector outlines.
Scene: elevated 3/4 overhead gameplay-like view over a medieval courtyard. On the RIGHT half, the actual squat tan stone tower with crenellations, narrow windows, arched brown door, moss seams and two low side buttresses. The small nearly black hooded cloaked player stands outside its door facing approaching green goblins from the lower right foreground, protecting the tower, clearly allied with it. The player has the simple dark faceless silhouette in the gameplay screenshot, not a shiny knight, no invented huge sword. Two or three small goblins with green pointed ears and brown clothing approach through a broken boundary wall and turquoise poisonous fog. Cracked golden paving, restrained gold inlay, a few candles and burgundy foliage match the game's map. Keep the LEFT 42% much quieter and darker, with low-contrast paving/fog/background silhouettes so editable game title and brief text can be overlaid. Put major tower silhouette around 69% horizontal position; keep it away from the top-right 8% which holds tool icons. Bright warm tower against cool outer fog, readable sprites. Whole rectangular image filled; not a circular map, no attack range rings, no diagram or target circles. NO TEXT, NO LOGO, NO UI, NO WATERMARK. Preserve the game's actual art identity and small-character proportions. This is a background art asset, title will be added separately in PowerPoint.
