# ToxicBarrier — Backstory

The first warning was the silence.

One autumn night, a poisonous fog erupted from beneath the streets of a medieval kingdom. It poured through cellar doors and rose from the wells. By dawn, the roads had vanished beneath it, and the bells of the surrounding villages had fallen silent.

Those who escaped spoke of creatures moving through the abandoned settlements. Goblins occupied the ruined streets. Beyond the city walls, soldiers buried centuries earlier clawed their way out of the earth. No one knew what had broken beneath the kingdom, or why the fog had brought the dead back with it.

At the heart of the city, an ancient watchtower still burned. Its warding flame held back the poison and struck the creatures that approached. The remaining survivors found shelter within its walls, but the tower had stood neglected for generations. Its stonework was failing, and its light weakened with every assault.

You are the last defender willing to leave its protection.

Wrapped in a dark cloak, you venture into the ruins to recover materials and restore the tower. Each journey gives it a better chance of surviving the next wave. Each moment away gives the creatures another chance to reach it.

Beyond the courtyard lie the poisoned streets and the outer burial grounds, where something is gathering the dead.

The tower cannot stand without you. You cannot survive without its light.
